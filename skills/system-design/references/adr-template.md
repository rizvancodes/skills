# Decision record template

One decision per record, after Michael Nygard's
[architecture decision records](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions).
Number records in order and never rewrite an agreed one: a changed decision gets a new record that
supersedes the old.

```markdown
# <number>. <decision, as a short statement>

Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Status: agreed | superseded by <number>

## Context

What forced the choice, and the constraints from step 1 it was checked against.

## Decision

What was decided, in a sentence or two. For a big choice, the options compared and the sources.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and the check that enforces it, if a program can decide
it.

## Open questions

- <question>
```
